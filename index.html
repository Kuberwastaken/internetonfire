how this site works

Confirmed: primary evidence supports the description. Reported: attributed reporting. Disputed: material disagreement. Needs evidence: not substantiated. Event dates and publication dates are different. An outage is not proof of a breach.

X posts preserve what was said, not proof that it happened. Corrections sit beside each entry and in git history. No private messages, leaked records, credentials or exploit payloads belong here. This is an incident index, not an emergency alert feed.
