What earns a place here

Follow the source

Original advisories and incident reports first. Reporting adds context. X posts and replies preserve what was said, not proof that it happened.

Keep the uncertainty

Confirmed means primary evidence supports the description. Reported means attributed reporting. Disputed means conflicting evidence. Needs evidence means we haven't substantiated the claim.

Use real dates

Publication, disclosure and event dates are different. Unknown dates stay unknown. No secret keys, leaked records, exploit payloads or victim data belong here.

Correct in the open

Corrections sit beside the incident. The repository keeps the history. A breach, an outage and a patched vulnerability aren't the same thing.

Add an incident

  1. Find a source and the date it was published. Include the vendor response when available.
  2. Open an incident report, or edit the JSON in your fork and send a pull request.
  3. Reviewers check sources, dates and wording before merging. Community reactions help prioritise review.
